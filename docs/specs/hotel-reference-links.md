## Important Note

Boutique Group’s properties are presented as a development portfolio rather than necessarily as fully operating hotels. Its official site identifies The Red Palace, Tuwaiq Palace, and Al Hamra Palace as palace projects.

For Riwaq, prioritize the operating properties as practical references for housekeeping workflows, guest services, heritage maintenance, and guest-experience standards. Use Boutique Group and Diriyah as strategic heritage-luxury references rather than assuming all listed properties are currently operational.
